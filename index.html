Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Bright Mesh.

What is Bright Mesh?
Bright Mesh is a multi-location audit platform for grocery and retail businesses. It lets you track daily sales, purchases, cash flow, expenses and stock — and automatically flags leakages, anomalies and red flags so you can act before money disappears.
How do I get started?
Contact your administrator to create a location and user account for you. Once you have a username and password, click Sign in at the top of the page, enter your details, and you'll land directly on your dashboard.
Who can create new users?
There are three ways users get created:

1. The Admin creates locations and their first manager.
2. Location managers can create additional users (Accountants, Cashiers) for their own location using the Team Management tab.
3. The Admin can view and edit every user, at every location.
What's the difference between Manager, Accountant and Cashier?
Manager — full control of the location, including creating users and closing months.

Accountant — can edit daily entries, purchases and expenses, but cannot close a month or manage users.

Cashier — view-only access. Cannot edit any data or see the audit log.
What are "red flags" and how do they work?
Red flags are automatic warnings the system raises when it detects suspicious patterns — like stock dropping more than 12% between closures, cash dropping more than 25%, duplicate purchases within 3 days, or exact round-number purchase amounts (a sign of fabricated invoices). The audit report sorts them into Red (critical), Amber (monitor), and Green (passed).
Is my data safe?
Yes. All data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore) — the same infrastructure trusted by thousands of businesses worldwide. Every entry is backed up in real time. Your login credentials and location data are never shared with other businesses on the platform.
Can I export my data?
Absolutely. From your dashboard, click Excel to download a multi-sheet spreadsheet with daily data, expenses, purchases and audit closures — or PDF for a formatted report you can send to an accountant or auditor.
Will I be logged out if I refresh the page?
No. Your session is remembered for the duration of your browser tab. You can safely refresh the page or navigate away and return without re-entering your password. If you close the browser tab, you will need to sign in again for security.
Can I change my password?
Yes. Once logged in, click Change Password in the top toolbar. You'll be asked for your current password and your new one. Choose something at least 6 characters long.
Is Bright Mesh free?
Yes — Bright Mesh is free to use. There are no subscription fees, no per-location charges, and no limits on the number of entries you can record. Reach out via the Contact us form to get started.
