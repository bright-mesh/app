Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Bright Mesh.

What is Bright Mesh?
Bright Mesh is a multi-location audit platform for grocery and retail businesses. It tracks daily sales, purchases, cash flow, expenses and stock — and automatically flags leakages, anomalies and red flags.
How does the daily closure work?
Each cashier opens a shift at the start of their working period (Morning, Afternoon or Evening). During and at the end of the shift, they record sales, mobile money received, expenses paid and the physical cash counted. The system then computes any Extra or Loss automatically and locks the shift when it's ended.
What can each role do?
Manager — full control: users, months, shifts, closures, reports.

Accountant — can edit daily entries, purchases, expenses; cannot close months or manage users.

Cashier — can start and end their own shift, record purchases, and view their own shift history.
What does the audit engine check?
The audit engine runs 15+ checks: stock flow mismatches, credit movement, cash shortages, low margins, negative net profit, shift differences, purchase variances and more. Each finding is rated Red (action required), Amber (review), or Green (passed).
What happens when a month ends?
At the start of a new month, Bright Mesh automatically closes the previous month and files it under Month History. Managers can also close all shifts for a month to see each cashier's total sales and total extra/loss.
Is my data safe?
Yes. All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). Every entry is backed up in real time. Your credentials and data are never shared with other businesses.
Is Bright Mesh free?
Yes — Bright Mesh is free to use. No subscription fees, no per-location charges, no limits on entries.
